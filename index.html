Who I Am

I’m someone who values clarity over noise and function over flash. I tend to work alone, not out of isolation, but because I like to understand systems end-to-end — from idea, to structure, to execution.

My background sits at the intersection of logic, creativity, and self-directed learning. I’m not chasing trends. I’m building understanding. These projects are artifacts of that process.

Why These Projects Exist

Most of the work here started because something bothered me: missing data, unclear comparisons, abstractions that hid more than they revealed. Rather than complain, I built.

Each repository is intentionally scoped. I prefer small, legible systems that do one thing well over sprawling platforms that do everything poorly.

How I Work

I’m comfortable being wrong early and precise later. I iterate quietly, refactor aggressively, and don’t ship until I understand what I’ve built. If something is here, it’s intentional.
